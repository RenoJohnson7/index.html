Health Guide

• Sugar Limit: The WHO recommends no more than 25g of added sugar per day.

• Caffeine: Most sodas contain 30-50mg of caffeine—about half a cup of coffee.

• Energy Drinks: Can contain up to 160mg+ of caffeine. Monitor intake closely.
